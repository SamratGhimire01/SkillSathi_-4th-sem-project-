How It Works

A simple three-step process for secure digital credentialing.

1. Issue

Training centers issue secure digital certificates to skilled workers instantly.

2. Secure

Certificates are cryptographically hashed and stored securely on the ledger.

3. Verify

Employers scan the QR code to instantly verify authenticity and details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question? We've got answers.

What is SkillSathi?

SkillSathi is a digital platform for issuing and verifying skill certificates. We partner with accredited training centers in Nepal.

How do I verify a certificate?

Use the Verification section above to enter the ID or scan the QR code found on the certificate.

Is it secure?

Yes. Each certificate is backed by a unique digital signature, making it tamper-proof.
