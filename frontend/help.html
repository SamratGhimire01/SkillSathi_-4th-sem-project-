Frequently Asked Questions

How do I issue a certificate?
Go to the "Certificates" page and click "Issue New". Fill in the student details and click Submit.
Can I bulk upload data?
Yes! Log in as a Staff member to access the CSV Bulk Import tool in your workspace.
How do I change my company logo?
Navigate to Settings > Branding. Upload a PNG image for best results.
Is the data secure?
Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and secure hashing for all certificates.
How to verify a certificate?
Anyone can verify a certificate by scanning the QR code or visiting the verification page.
